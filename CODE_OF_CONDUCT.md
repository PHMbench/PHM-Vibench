# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PHM-Vibench maintainers and contributors are committed to a respectful,
welcoming, and technically constructive community. Participation should be free
from harassment regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of constructive participation include:

- using precise, respectful language;
- assuming good faith while reviewing evidence critically;
- giving and receiving actionable feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting different experience levels and backgrounds;
- keeping technical disagreement focused on code, data, methods, and evidence;
- protecting private, proprietary, and embargoed information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory comments;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberately misrepresenting test results, authorship, data provenance, or
  scientific evidence;
- retaliating against someone who raises a good-faith concern;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional open-source or research setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
project-managed communication spaces, and public events where a participant is
representing PHM-Vibench or PHMbench.

## Reporting a conduct concern

Do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue.

The project does not currently publish a dedicated conduct-reporting email. Use
one of these private paths:

1. contact a PHMbench organization owner or repository maintainer through an
   existing private channel;
2. use GitHub's **Report abuse** mechanism for conduct that violates GitHub's
   policies;
3. when no private project channel is available, open a non-sensitive issue that
   asks a maintainer to provide a private contact method, without naming people
   or describing the incident publicly.

Security vulnerabilities follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not this process.

Reports will be reviewed by maintainers who are not directly involved in the
reported incident whenever possible. No fixed response-time SLA is promised.
Maintainers should protect the reporter's privacy, document decisions, and avoid
retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request changes in behavior, issue a
warning, restrict participation, close discussions, or ban an account from
project-managed spaces. The response should be proportionate to the behavior,
its impact, prior incidents, and the safety of the community.

A maintainer who is the subject of a report must not make the final enforcement
decision alone.
