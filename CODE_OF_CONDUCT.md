# Code of Conduct

PHM-Vibench is committed to a professional, respectful, and technically honest
collaboration environment for users, contributors, reviewers, and maintainers.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with respect, patience, and empathy;
- focus criticism on code, evidence, design, and outcomes rather than people;
- make review feedback specific and actionable;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes when evidence changes;
- respect differences in experience, language, background, identity, and research
  perspective;
- protect private information, credentials, unpublished data, and embargoed
  security details;
- represent implementation status, test results, authorship, citations, and
  performance evidence accurately;
- help keep issues and pull requests within their stated scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, or personal attacks;
- trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately hostile review behavior;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring contributors to reveal private data, credentials, or unpublished
  results;
- knowingly fabricating tests, benchmarks, citations, authorship, compatibility,
  or project status;
- using generated content, copied code, data, or model artifacts without review,
  attribution, or applicable license information;
- retaliating against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release processes, project-managed communication channels, and public situations
where a participant is representing PHM-Vibench.

Technical disagreement is expected. Maintainers may redirect, close, hide, or
remove content that is off-topic, unsafe, abusive, misleading, or incompatible
with the repository's contribution requirements.

## Reporting a concern

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

Use the private reporting path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and state
that the report concerns community conduct rather than a software vulnerability.
If GitHub private reporting is not available, contact a repository maintainer
through their GitHub profile to establish a private channel before sharing
sensitive details.

Include, when safe and relevant:

- links or timestamps;
- a factual description of what occurred;
- people or project spaces affected;
- supporting screenshots or logs;
- immediate safety or privacy concerns;
- the outcome being requested.

Maintainers will limit access to the report to people needed for review. A
maintainer who is directly involved in a report should not be the sole decision
maker for that report.

## Enforcement

Possible actions include clarification, content removal, a private warning,
temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban from project spaces.
The response should be proportionate to severity, impact, repetition, and safety
risk.

No fixed response deadline is promised. PHM-Vibench is an open-source research
project, and maintainer availability varies. Urgent threats to personal safety
should be reported to the appropriate local service rather than relying on the
repository.
