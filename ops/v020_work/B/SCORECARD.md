# GOAL B Scorecard

Use this scorecard after every A cycle.

## 1. Evidence quality — 20
- 20: every important claim has command, SHA, file, or validation evidence.
- 15: most claims supported; minor gaps remain.
- 10: key claims need checking.
- 0: decisions are based on unsupported assumptions.

## 2. Safety and reversibility — 20
- 20: each risky step has archive protection and rollback point.
- 15: plan is mostly reversible.
- 10: rollback path is incomplete.
- 0: irreversible action is proposed.

## 3. Release focus — 15
- 15: changes directly improve v0.2.0 release readiness.
- 10: mostly relevant with small distractions.
- 5: mixed focus.
- 0: unrelated expansion.

## 4. PHM user value — 15
- 15: new users can understand, run, and extend the repo more easily.
- 10: user value is plausible but not proven.
- 5: only maintainer value.
- 0: user experience worsens.

## 5. Scientific reproducibility — 15
- 15: config, validation, and evidence chain are clearer.
- 10: no regression but limited improvement.
- 5: reproducibility unclear.
- 0: silent or ambiguous behavior introduced.

## 6. Maintainability — 10
- 10: patch slices are small and reviewable.
- 5: some mixed concerns.
- 0: large unclear patch.

## 7. Next-action clarity — 5
- 5: A has a concrete next 50-minute plan.
- 3: plan is usable but broad.
- 0: next step unclear.
